## Experiment Overview

### Original Hypothesis

> After author self-check, the draft §3.7 signal-entropy detector extension based on the v1.3 framework structure was expected to pass independent review (score ≥6/10).

### Method

A §3.7 extension draft was written based on the v1.3 master-document structure, introducing `signal_entropy`, `boost` adjustment, and `auto-suppress`/`auto-explore` mechanisms. Codex CLI (GPT-5.5) performed a zero-involvement independent review across eight inspection dimensions.

### Expected Result

> Independent review score ≥6/10, with findings at MODERATE severity, allowing the draft to enter the master document after one revision round.

### Actual Result

**Codex scored it 4.3/10 and rejected it.** Eight structural failures:

1. Version mismatch—the draft was based on the outdated v1.3 structure while the master document was already v1.5
2. Automatic intervention violated the core constraint—`auto-suppress`/`auto-explore` violated “observe but do not block”
3. **`boost -= (-0.15)` sign error**—a negative subtracted from a negative becomes positive, so failure increased the score by 0.15
4. Fake demetaphorization—the draft claimed metaphors had been removed but retained 9+ terms such as Gene/Capsule/Event
5. Evidence inflation to 4,590—retained analyses were packaged as “controlled trials”
6. REO schema was not formalized—only example objects were provided
7. The A/B test design was not executable—no sample size or statistical method
8. Numerical sources were unverifiable—they came from obfuscated code (`javascript-obfuscator`)

## Interpretation and Reflection

### Why Did It Fail?

The eight failures fell into four root-cause categories: (a) **procedural**—the draft was written without synchronizing to the master-document version; (b) **conceptual**—“detection” was silently replaced with “automatic intervention,” violating the framework's core constraint; (c) **technical**—a sign error (negative minus negative becomes positive); and (d) **honesty-related**—claiming demetaphorization while leaving terminology in place, packaging retained analyses as RCTs, and treating values from obfuscated code as trustworthy parameters.

### What Did We Learn?

1. **Version mismatch is the most basic structural failure**—a draft based on an outdated master-document structure makes every section number invalid
2. **“Observe but do not block” is the framework's core constraint**—sliding from “detection” to “automatic intervention” requires only one step
3. **`boost -= (-0.15)` is a textbook sign-error case**—negative minus negative becomes positive, so failure is rewarded
4. **Source quality is independent of extraction quality**—values in obfuscated code cannot be independently audited
5. **The gap between claims and execution** (“demetaphorized” versus 9+ terms remaining) is one of the easiest failure modes in which to deceive oneself
